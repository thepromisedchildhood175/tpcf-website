How you can help

Every contribution helps create brighter futures for children facing hardship.

Donate

Your gift will fund comfort kits, birthday celebrations, and mentorship for children facing medical and socioeconomic hardship in the Houston area.

Give once or monthly. Every gift is processed securely by Zeffy, and 100% of it goes to TPCF. Zeffy may ask if you'd like to add an optional tip to support their free platform; you can set it to $0.

Open the donation form

The Promised Childhood Foundation is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization. Donations are tax-deductible to the extent allowed by law.

Volunteer

Give your time to help children feel supported. Students, groups, and companies are welcome.

  • Pack and deliver Firefly Comfort Kits
  • Write encouragement and birthday cards
  • Help run fundraising events
  • Mentor a student (coming later)
