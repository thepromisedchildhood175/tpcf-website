Frequently asked questions

Is my donation tax-deductible?

Yes. The Promised Childhood Foundation is recognized by the IRS as a 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization, so donations are tax-deductible to the extent allowed by law. Please keep your donation receipt for your records.

Where does TPCF work?

We're based in Houston, Texas, and focused on supporting children and families across the Houston area.

How can my organization partner with TPCF?

Fill out the partnership form or email admin@thepromisedchildhood.org. We work with hospitals, clinics, schools, businesses, and community groups.
