About TPCF

Our story

Why we started The Promised Childhood Foundation, and what we believe.

Why we started

Growing up, we witnessed how illness can affect far more than a person's health. Families facing medical challenges often encounter emotional stress, financial hardship, and disruptions to a child's education and development. These experiences showed us that many children face obstacles beyond their control, limiting opportunities that should be available to every child.

We founded The Promised Childhood Foundation to support children facing medical and socioeconomic hardship and to help ensure that difficult circumstances do not define their future.

Our mission

The Promised Childhood Foundation is dedicated to supporting children facing medical and socioeconomic hardship by providing resources, mentorship, and community-driven programs that preserve the joy, dignity, and potential of childhood.

What we believe

Every child deserves the opportunity to thrive.
Support should extend beyond medical treatment alone.
Communities have the power to create meaningful change in children's lives.
Hope and encouragement are essential parts of healing and growth.
A child's circumstances should never determine their potential.
