Privacy policy

Last updated: [DATE OF LAUNCH]

What we collect

We only collect information you choose to send us through the forms on this site: your name, email, and phone number if you provide it, and the details you write in your message. When you request a Firefly Kit or nominate a child, we ask for the child's first name, an age range, and optionally a hospital, clinic, or school. We ask you not to share medical details or diagnoses.

How we use it

We use what you send only to respond to you and to run our programs, such as delivering a kit, scheduling volunteers, or following up on a partnership. We do not sell, rent, or trade your information, and we do not use it for advertising.

Children's information

Information about a child must be submitted by a parent or guardian, or by someone with their permission. We keep it only as long as we need it for the program it was shared for. A parent or guardian can ask us to delete it at any time.

Services we use

Our website is hosted by GitHub Pages, and our forms are processed by Formspree, which delivers your submission to our email. These services handle data under their own privacy policies.

Your choices

To see, correct, or delete information you've sent us, or to unsubscribe from updates, email admin@thepromisedchildhood.org.
